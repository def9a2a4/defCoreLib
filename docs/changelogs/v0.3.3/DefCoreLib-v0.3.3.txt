# DefCoreLib v0.3.3

Two new frame blocks join the Casing, plus the biggest glue/contraption upgrade since glue itself: mechanisms now carry glued machines as one rigid body, a rotator can hang from the ceiling, and a rotation-network server hang under redstone pulse storms is fixed.

- **Gearbox** and **Chassis** — two new mechanical frame blocks, in all 12 woods
- **Frame rework** — casings, gearboxes, and chassis auto-glue by matching wood, and can be brush-pinned rigidly to anything
- **Carried contraptions** — mechanisms now move glued machines (a chain hoist rides a rotator, load and all)
- **Ceiling rotator** — a rotator can mount under a block as a swinging hinge
- **Ratchet** — a new one-way rotation valve
- **Stability** — fixes a server-thread hang under comparator/observer pulse storms; steadier mover speeds

## Blocks

- **Gearbox** — an omnidirectional power hub: it passes power in every direction, on all six faces, but not spin direction — each side settles its own rotation. Split one input into several outputs, or turn a corner without a gear's counter-rotation.
- **Chassis** — a slime-reach casing: it grabs every adjacent movable block except honey, so a moving contraption drags its whole payload with no glue brush, while still joining a same-wood casing frame.
- **Ratchet** — a one-way valve for rotation: it drives one way and freewheels (disconnects) the other, instead of jamming. Wrench to set the direction, sneak-wrench to inspect.

## Frame glue

- Casing, gearbox, and chassis are one **frame** family keyed by wood — they auto-glue only to same-wood frame blocks. The glue brush pins one rigidly to anything (any wood, or a plain block). *Mixed-wood casing frames built before 0.3.3 no longer auto-glue on their own — see the mech notes.*

## Contraptions

- Movers (piston, rotator, drawbridge, minecart, chain hoist) now carry **nested glued machines** — a chain hoist glued onto a rotator swings as one body, head + chain + platform together.
- Rotators can mount under a block as a **ceiling hinge**, swinging the block above like a hatch.

## Stability

- Fixed a server-thread hang under redstone pulse storms: the rotation network and custom rails now coalesce their recomputes to once per tick, and the plugin's own block writes no longer feed their own physics loop.
- Mover speeds slowed to steadier, more readable rates.

See the per-plugin **mech** notes for full detail, recipes, and advancements.
