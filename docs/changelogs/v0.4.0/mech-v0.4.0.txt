# mech v0.4.0

Two new frame blocks — the **Gearbox** and the **Chassis** — and a new **Ratchet** valve, plus the biggest glue/contraption upgrade yet: mechanisms now carry glued machines as one rigid body and a rotator can hang from the ceiling. This release also rebalances several core recipes, adds an in-game item catalog (`/defcorelib catalog`), and fixes a rotation-network server hang under redstone pulse storms.

## New blocks

- **Gearbox** (all 12 woods) — an omnidirectional power hub. On each of its six faces it couples to an aligned shaft, gear, or another gearbox and passes **power** through, but **not spin direction** — each side of a gearbox settles its own rotation. Split one input into several directions at once, or bridge a shaft around a corner without the counter-rotation a gear introduces. An omni machine — a suction hopper, mechanical dispenser, or piston — can draw power from a gearbox on **any of its six faces**. It's a frame block (see below): auto-glues only to same-wood frame blocks, brush-pin it to anything else. **Craft:** a Mechanical Casing in the centre, gears on three sides, a shaft on top — the casing's wood picks the variant.
- **Chassis** (all 12 woods) — a "super-casing" with slime reach: it grabs every adjacent **movable** block except honey, exactly like a slime block, so a moving contraption drags its whole payload with no glue brush — while still joining a same-wood casing frame. **Craft:** ring a Mechanical Casing with iron nuggets — the casing's wood picks the variant.
- **Ratchet** — a one-way rotation valve. It transmits rotation only while the shaft spins its **allowed** direction (CW/CCW); turned the other way it **freewheels** — fully disconnects, so the input keeps spinning while everything past it goes dead (no jam, no stall). **Wrench** to flip the allowed direction, **sneak-wrench** to inspect it (allowed direction + whether it's currently freewheeling). Dropped onto a line that's already turning, it adopts that direction, so it engages on placement and only freewheels once the line is reversed. The direction is stored in the block, so it survives being carried by a mechanism, and it acts as a plain shaft while riding one. **Craft:** a bearing over a reverser over a gear, iron pawls flanking the reverser, copper in the corners (all-mechanical, no redstone).

## Features

- **Frame blocks are wood-keyed** — casing, gearbox, and chassis are now one "frame" family: a frame block auto-glues only to adjacent frame blocks of the **same wood** (oak casing ↔ oak gearbox ↔ oak chassis, never oak ↔ birch). A plain block no longer auto-drags an adjacent frame — a frame rides only when a machine carries it directly, when it's bonded to a same-wood frame that rides, when a chassis or slime grabs it, or when it's brush-pinned.
- **Brush-pinning frames** — the glue brush now **pins** a frame block: the pin is stored and rides rigidly, and it *overrides* the wood gate, so you can pin two different woods together, or pin a frame to a plain block.
- **Movers carry nested machines** — piston, rotator, drawbridge, minecart, and chain hoist now carry glued **anchors**. Glue a chain hoist onto a rotator (or piston, cart, …) and it comes along: head, chain column, and hanging platform swing as one rigid body and reorient correctly on landing. A carried drill, placer, or fan aims along its own facing rotated by the mechanism — an outward-pointing drill stays pointing outward through the whole motion — and a carried rotator keeps its chosen open angle.
- **Ceiling rotator** — a rotator mounted under a block now hangs as a ceiling hinge, swinging the block above it like a hatch (Y axis) — the floor door, upside down.
- **Mounted drill mines mid-swing** — a drill carried by a rotator now bores through the whole arc at any angle, not only at the 90° stops, and breaks at 2× speed while swinging since it only grazes each block.
- **Steadier mover speeds** — the chain hoist and piston floor at about 0.5 block/s, and the rotator runs 5–90°/s.
- **Wrench-inspect a moving rotation network** — right-click a shaft, gear, or machine on an *assembled* mechanism (rotator, drawbridge, minecart, …) with the wrench to read its live power supply/demand, block count, spin direction, and jam state, with the network members highlighted — the same readout the wrench gives a rotation network in the world, now available while it rides a contraption.
- **Movers mow plants in their path** — a mechanical piston or chain hoist now breaks the grass, ferns, flowers, and similar fragile blocks in the cells ahead of it (dropping them) instead of stalling against them.

## Fixes

- **Server hang under pulse storms** — the plugin's own block writes no longer re-trigger their own physics/recalc feedback loop, and reactive-neighbor and custom-rail updates now coalesce to a single recompute per network/rail per tick under a comparator or observer pulse storm. A throwing reactive handler is isolated and logged once, never fatal to the tick.
- **Carried-hoist glue integrity** — a carried hoist that would be tipped off its vertical column (a horizontal-axis drawbridge) or grabbed mid-stroke is now **refused** rather than corrupted, and a hoist's stored platform glue is cleared whenever its chain length changes out of band (a link mined, placed, or blown up, or the hoist landing with a shortened chain), so its offsets can never mis-reference.
- **Seed oil returns its bottle** — burning a Seed Oil (a bottled fuel) in a fuel-fed machine now returns the empty glass bottle to the fuel storage (dropping it at the block if the storage is full) instead of consuming the container.
- **Bare shafts stay bare when carried** — a shaft wrenched to its bare **chain** form now stays bare after a rotator (or any mechanism) swings it, instead of silently reverting to the encased shaft on landing. A shaft carried while encased still lands encased.
- **Carried blocks keep their decoration** — a block's block-entity data now survives a mechanism move: a sign's text/colour/glow, a container's custom name, a lectern's book, a jukebox's disc, decorated-pot sherds, a command block's command, and the like. A block also stays waterlogged only where it lands back into a water source.
- **Chunk-boundary crash** — fixed a server error when a mechanism minecart crossed a chunk boundary while it was being ticked.
- **Bare shafts ride bare** — a shaft carried in its bare **chain** form now renders as a spinning chain while the contraption moves, instead of showing the boxed shaft head until it lands.
- **Carried banners survive** — vanilla, **large**, and **huge** banners on a mechanism now keep their patterns, tier, and orientation as it moves and re-land with the contraption, instead of drifting to the pivot and then vanishing when it's taken apart. A vanilla banner on a mechanism also no longer shows a duplicate floating "ghost" copy.
- **Throttle keeps its level when carried** — a throttle lever moved by a mechanism re-lands at its set level instead of resetting to 0.
- **No blocks lost when stopping the server** — a still-assembled mechanism carrying a **throttle lever** now restores its full structure on `/stop`; previously the shutdown could abort partway through re-placing the blocks, permanently losing every block that hadn't landed yet.
- **Cleaner shutdown** — stopping the server no longer prints a misleading "failed to disassemble on shutdown … removing entities without block restore" warning for a mechanism resting beside a rotation part or a custom rail. The blocks were in fact always restored; the warning was spurious (and a carried bare **chain shaft** could re-land in its encased form).
- **Engines keep their fuel when carried** — a lit engine/burner moved by a mechanism no longer lands empty; its remaining fuel rides along.
- **Machine facings ride true** — a dynamo, boiler, burner, pump, mechanical dispenser, piston head, or cart rail carried by a mechanism now keeps its oriented shell/head/eye while it moves and on landing, instead of briefly snapping to a default direction in transit and only correcting itself when it lands.
- **Floor heads keep their rotation while moving** — a floor-placed custom head (a redstone motor, and the like) now renders at its real facing while a mechanism carries it, instead of snapping to a default orientation in transit and only correcting itself on landing.
- **Light blocks no longer block a mechanism** — a light-emitting head's invisible light block no longer counts as a wall that stops a mechanical piston or chain hoist; the stroke moves through it cleanly. (A stationary light head whose light block is overrun by another block goes dark until its chunk reloads.)

## Recipes

- **Shaft** now yields **2** per craft.
- **Bearing**, **Millstone**, and **Sieve** take a **shaft** as their power intake in place of a lightning rod.
- **Engine** and **Redstone Motor** gain a **gear** in the base of the recipe.
- **Sieve** panning drop rates rebalanced.

## Catalog

- Browse every mech block — with recipes — in-game via `/defcorelib catalog` (casings, gearboxes, and chassis sort to the end so the machines come first).
- **Pick-block** — middle-clicking a mech block or a mechanism minecart no longer mints a free copy in survival or adventure; it only selects one you already carry (creative still gives it).

## Compatibility

- **Mixed-wood casing frames** built before 0.4.0 no longer auto-glue on their own. Rebuild a frame in a single wood, or brush-pin the mixed joints.

## Advancements

- New craft nodes: the **Ratchet** ("One-Way Street") and the **Gearbox** ("Every Which Way"). The Chassis reuses the existing Mechanical Casing craft node.

## Internal

- Rotation-network reactive recalcs coalesce through a per-tick flush drain, and the read-only lock/ratchet-direction predicates are memoized per recalc — collapsing a comparator storm from many full O(component) rebuilds to one.
