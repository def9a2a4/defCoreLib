headsmith v0.4.0

INITIAL RELEASE (NEW)
HeadSmith is a new companion plugin that adds a large library of decorative
player-head blocks to your world. Everything lives as DefCoreLib data under
the "headsmith" namespace, so the heads register, craft, place, and break
like any other def block, and show up in the core item catalog. It needs
DefCoreLib installed; without the core, the heads simply don't load.

HEADS
- A big catalog of decorative heads organized into families: the alphabet
  (letters, numbers, greek, cyrillic, runes, galactic, zodiac, arrows, and
  symbols across many materials), mini-blocks, candles, books, barrels,
  bundles, buckets, pumpkins, chalices, bottles, and more.
- A cross-cutting color axis groups every black / white / red / … head under
  its color, so you can browse by shape or by color.
- Browse them all in-game with the core catalog: `/defcorelib catalog` groups
  every HeadSmith head by category, and each dye-color node shows that color's
  mini-wool as its icon.
- Get them in survival by crafting: most heads are carved in the stonecutter
  from mini-blocks (for example, a mini birch-planks block cuts into birch
  letters), with some shaped and shapeless recipes too.
- HeadSmith recipes stay out of the vanilla recipe book by default. Run
  `/headsmith recipes give` to reveal them in your book, or `take` to hide
  them again.

CHIMNEY HEADS (NEW)
- Chimney heads in a range of materials (stone, brick, cobblestone,
  sandstone, nether brick, metal, and more). Light one with flint & steel and
  it puffs a steady rising campfire-smoke plume; right-click again to snuff it
  out with an extinguish sound. Chimneys give off smoke only — they emit no
  light.

COMMANDS
- `/headsmith give <id> [amount]` - give yourself a head by id (op).
- `/headsmith recipes <give|take> [player]` - reveal or hide HeadSmith recipes
  in a recipe book (anyone may run it for themselves; an operator can run it
  for another player).
- `/headsmith migrate` - adopt already-placed heads from the old standalone
  HeadSmith plugin (op).
