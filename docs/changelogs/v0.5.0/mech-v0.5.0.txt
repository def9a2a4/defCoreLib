# mech v0.5.0

The first release since 0.3.2 (0.4.0 was never published, so everything here is new to you). New frame blocks (Gearbox, Chassis), a Ratchet valve, and ship propulsion for BlockShips — Propellers, a Thruster, a Reaction wheel and Wheels. Contraptions now carry glued machines, cushions, item frames and paintings; engines burn fuel only for the power used; and many item-loss and crash fixes.

## Update notes

- **Run Paper or Purpur 1.21.11 or newer** (26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 are supported). Mechanism needs the datapack-discovery bootstrap API; `api-version` is still `1.21`, so an older server loads the plugin and fails later instead of refusing it at startup.
- **Bare shafts become waxed copper chains** (were iron chains). Worlds convert as chunks load, keeping each shaft's axis; builds decorated around iron chains will look different.
- **Windmills carried on a mechanism supply half power** — 0 / 2 / 7 instead of 1 / 5 / 15 (a small windmill aboard gives nothing). Power is all-or-nothing, so a contraption running near break-even off its own windmills will stop: add a windmill or drop a consumer before you next assemble it.
- **Power blocks weigh something on a ship** — engine 8, steam piston 6, windmills 2 / 6 / 15 (they weighed as little as wool). A ship near its float or lift limit may need rebalancing when next assembled.
- **Mixed-wood casing frames no longer auto-glue.** Rebuild a frame in one wood, or brush-pin the mixed joints.
- **Engines burn only for the power used** — a network with idle machines burns little or nothing. To keep the old fixed burn, set `engines: { burn-on-load: false }` in `plugins/DefCoreLib/config/fuel.yml`.
- **Recipe changes:** the Shaft yields 2; Bearing, Millstone and Sieve take a shaft instead of a lightning rod; Engine and Redstone Motor gain a gear in the base.
- **Sieve panning yields much less.** 23 of 35 drop rates came down, most by half: sand → clay 14% → 1%, red sand → gold nuggets 12% → 2%, gravel → gold nuggets 5% → 1%, dirt → wheat seeds 10% → 3%. Sand no longer yields redstone. The soul sand table, gravel's coal/lapis/diamond, sand's kelp and quartz, and dirt's clay ball, bone meal and stick are unchanged; gravel can now yield redstone and red sand yields iron nuggets and raw iron. Size sieve farms for the new rates.

## New features

- **Gearbox** (all 12 woods) — an omnidirectional power hub: it passes power and spin on all six faces. Sources on different sides never jam, and an arm with no source of its own follows the nearest one. Split one input several ways, or turn a corner without a gear's counter-rotation; an omni machine (suction hopper, mechanical dispenser, piston) can draw from it on any face. **Craft:** a Mechanical Casing with gears on three sides and a shaft on top; the casing's wood picks the variant.
- **Chassis** (all 12 woods) — a casing with slime reach: it grabs every adjacent movable block except honey, so a contraption drags its payload with no glue brush, and still joins a same-wood frame. **Craft:** a Mechanical Casing ringed with iron nuggets.
- **Ratchet** — a one-way rotation valve: it passes rotation only while the line spins its allowed direction and freewheels (disconnects, no jam) the other way. Wrench to flip, sneak-wrench to inspect. Placed on a turning line it adopts that direction. **Craft:** a bearing over a reverser over a gear, iron flanking the reverser, copper in the corners.
- **Propeller, Large propeller, Huge propeller** — ship thrust (5 / 10 / 20 power), crafted from the matching windmill, keeping its banner design. They do nothing in the world; a BlockShips vessel reads them. Mount direction picks the role: fore/aft adds speed, sideways adds turning, up or down adds lift. **Craft:** a windmill in the centre, iron corners, redstone edges.
- **Thruster** — burns fuel instead of rotation power, so it works on a ship with no network. It burns only while pushing the ship (throttle, steering, or lift holding a flier up); parked, docked or placed, it burns nothing. Its plume shows while it burns and follows the nozzle. Mount it on a wall or ceiling — a floor thruster's plume has nowhere to go. **Craft:** a Fan, four iron ingots, two blaze rods and a blast furnace.
- **Reaction wheel** — a gyroscope that turns the ship whichever way it's mounted, for 1 power. The only turning a floor mount gives. **Craft:** two redstone either side of an iron block, over a bearing.
- **Wheel, Large wheel, Huge wheel** (all 12 woods) — land-vehicle wheels for BlockShips (2 / 5 / 10 power). A hull with wheels on its left and right drives on land, climbing 1 / 1.5 / 2-block steps by size; wheels roll at road speed even unpowered. Wall placement only; a wheel takes power through the block it hangs on, and only while its vehicle is assembled — a placed wheel draws no power and stays still. **Craft:** four planks around a Rotator; a larger wheel is four planks around the size below, in the same wood.
- **Carried contraptions** — piston, rotator, drawbridge, minecart and chain hoist carry glued machines: a chain hoist glued to a rotator swings with its chain and platform, and a carried drill, placer or fan keeps aiming along its own facing.
- **Cushions, item frames and paintings ride along** — a 26.3 cushion keeps its rider seated through the move; frames keep their item and re-hang on the turned face; paintings re-hang too. With nowhere to land (a protected cell, a block in the way, a painting rolled onto its side) they drop as items.
- **Ceiling mounts** — a rotator under a block swings it like a hatch; propellers and windmills of every tier hang under a block on a shaft running up into it, working exactly like a floor mount.
- **Frame blocks are wood-keyed** — casing, gearbox and chassis auto-glue only to same-wood frame blocks, and a plain block no longer drags an adjacent frame. The glue brush **pins** a frame to anything, any wood.
- **Glued selections up to 1024 blocks** (was 256).
- **Engines burn fuel for the power actually used** — a machine burns fuel only while it is working (a millstone, press or sieve processing, a drill boring, a pump pumping, a placer placing, a suction hopper catching items, a dispenser firing, a mechanical piston or chain hoist moving), so an idle line burns nothing, half load burns half as fast, and windmills and water wheels cover the load first. Ship propellers, wheels and thrusters burn by how hard BlockShips uses them, and nothing on a docked ship. Steam pistons drink boiler water the same way.
- **Fuel settings are admin-editable** — copy the `fuel`, `engines`, `thruster`, `steam` or `load` sections of rotation-config.yml into `plugins/DefCoreLib/config/fuel.yml`.
- **Mounted drill mines mid-swing** — a drill on a rotator bores through the whole arc, at 2× speed while swinging.
- **Heavier contraptions move slower** — speed scales with the carried blocks' mass, not their count.
- **Wrench-inspect a moving network** — the wrench readout (supply, demand, direction, jam, highlighted members) works on a network riding a contraption.
- **Movers mow plants** — a mechanical piston or chain hoist breaks grass, flowers and similar blocks ahead of it instead of stalling.
- **Smoother animations** — spinning parts tween between frames instead of snapping.
- **Catalog and help** — every mech block, with recipes, in `/defcorelib catalog`; `/mech` and `/mech help` show the commands and links (`/mech advance` still needs `mech.admin`). Pick-block selects a mech block or mechanism cart you carry instead of minting one in survival.
- **Advancements** — "One-Way Street" (craft a Ratchet) and "Every Which Way" (craft a Gearbox).

## Fixes

- **Log out while seated on a contraption** and you're back in your seat when you rejoin, if it's still there; otherwise you rejoin where you left.
- **Chain hoist broken while lowering** lost up to 47 chains; the unused chain now drops with the hoist.
- **Placer emptied shulker boxes** — a filled shulker box was placed empty; names, banner patterns and head skins were lost the same way, and doors, beds and tall plants were placed as one half. The placer now leaves those in its source.
- **Copper chest beside a Boiler or Water Wheel** joined it into a double chest; breaking the machine wiped the neighbour's contents. They no longer pair, and breaking one never touches the other's items.
- **Chain pulley glued into a mechanism duplicated chains**, and breaking one end while the other sat in an unloaded chunk could refund twice.
- **Carried blocks keep their contents and decoration** — a lectern's book, a decorated pot's item and sherds, sign text, a container's name, a jukebox's disc; a mechanical dispenser's contents ride along instead of dropping.
- **Carried banners survive** — vanilla, large and huge banners keep their patterns, tier and orientation and re-land with the contraption, with no ghost copy.
- **Engines and burners keep their fuel** — a part-burned item survives being carried and restarts, and a landed engine no longer gets back the fuel it had at take-off.
- **A burned lava bucket or seed-oil bottle returns its container** — stacked onto one already in the machine, otherwise dropped beside it (an engine aboard keeps it in its storage instead of dropping it overboard).
- **Surplus power costs fuel** — a millstone, press or sieve running bigger batches with spare power pays for the extra cycles it actually ran, and a mechanical piston or chain hoist pays for the speed spare power gives it.
- **The suction hopper leaves protected items alone** (shop displays), and plugins can veto its pickups like a vanilla hopper's.
- **Steam piston parts go out when it's broken**, and its status readout no longer says "no fuel" while the burner holds fuel.
- **Machines at the edge of the loaded area** no longer load the chunk next door.
- **A parked contraption's drill stops cracking its block.**
- **A machine placed shortly before a crash** (water wheel, boiler, mechanical dispenser) is recognised again instead of coming back as a plain block.
- **Bare shafts stay bare** while carried and on landing.
- **Heads keep their facing on a moving mechanism** — wall heads rendered backwards, floor heads snapped to a default, and dynamos, boilers, burners, pumps, dispensers, piston heads and cart rails briefly showed the wrong direction in transit.
- **Drawbridges turn what they swing the right way** — a wall-mounted rotator landed its blocks as if it had turned flat, so stairs, logs, slabs, trapdoors, levers, shafts and gears came down facing the wrong way (a shaft could even join the wrong line, spinning backwards). Blocks now turn with the swing; a torch, sign, banner or head tipped onto a floor or wall takes its standing or wall form, and anything with no sideways form (a door, bed, plant, rail or carpet) drops as an item.
- **Throttle levers keep their level** when carried.
- **No blocks lost when stopping the server** with a contraption carrying a throttle lever assembled; a misleading shutdown warning is gone.
- **Server hang under pulse storms** — reactive and custom-rail updates coalesce to one recompute per tick, and a throwing handler is logged once instead of stalling the tick.
- **Carried hoists stay intact** — a hoist that would be tipped off its column or grabbed mid-stroke is refused, and its platform glue is cleared when its chain length changes out of band.
- **Light blocks no longer stop a piston or hoist.**
- **Chunk-boundary crash** when a mechanism minecart crossed a chunk boundary mid-tick.
- **Contraptions reliably survive a crash** — a parked mechanism (and a BlockShips ship) is rediscovered from its own entities when its chunk loads.
- **Steadier mover speeds** — the chain hoist and piston move at least 0.5 block/s; the rotator turns 5–90°/s.
