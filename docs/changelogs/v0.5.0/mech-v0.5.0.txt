# Mechanism v0.5.0

The first release since 0.3.2 (0.4.0 was never published, so everything here is new to you). New frame blocks (Gearbox, Chassis), a Ratchet valve, and ship propulsion for BlockShips — Propellers, a Thruster, a Reaction wheel and Wheels. Contraptions now carry glued machines, cushions, item frames and paintings; engines burn fuel only for the power used; and many item-loss and crash fixes.

## Update notes

- **Run Paper or Purpur 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 or 26.3, with DefCoreLib 0.5.0.** DefCoreLib needs it too (copper-chain shafts don't exist before 1.21.9). `api-version` is still `1.21`, so an older server loads the plugins and then errors instead of refusing them at startup.
- **Ship blocks need BlockShips 0.0.18 or newer** — propellers, the thruster, the reaction wheel and wheels do nothing without it.
- **Contraptions move slower.** Speed now follows the load's mass (planks 2, stone 3, obsidian 10 per block) instead of block count, and the limits came down: a rotator turns 5–90°/s (was 30–180°/s), and a chain hoist or mechanical piston can go as slow as 0.5 block/s (was 1.6). Wood and stone builds run 2–3× slower than in 0.3.2; add power, and retune redstone timed around a mover.
- **Frames only drag frames.** A plain block no longer pulls an adjacent casing, gearbox or chassis along, so an existing contraption may leave part of its frame behind; glue-brush the joint. **Mixed-wood frames no longer auto-glue** either: rebuild a frame in one wood, or brush-pin the mixed joints.
- **Bare shafts become waxed copper chains** (were iron chains). Worlds convert as chunks load, keeping each shaft's axis; builds decorated around iron chains will look different.
- **Windmills carried on a mechanism supply half power** — 0 / 2 / 7 instead of 1 / 5 / 15 (a small windmill aboard gives nothing). Power is all-or-nothing, so a contraption running near break-even off its own windmills will stop: add a windmill or drop a consumer before you next assemble it.
- **Power blocks weigh something on a ship** — engine 8, steam piston 6, windmills 2 / 6 / 15 (they weighed as little as wool). A ship near its float or lift limit may need rebalancing when next assembled.
- **Engines burn only for the power used** — a network with idle machines burns little or nothing. To keep the old fixed burn, set `engines: { burn-on-load: false }` in `plugins/DefCoreLib/config/fuel.yml`.
- **Carried blocks land waterlogged only in water** — a contraption no longer drags water onto dry land, so a waterlogged block carried out of water lands dry.
- **Recipe changes:** the Shaft yields 2; the Gear, Millstone and Sieve take a shaft instead of a lightning rod; the Engine and Redstone Motor gain a gear in the base.
- **Sieve panning yields much less.** 23 of the 36 drop rates came down, most by half or more: sand → clay 14% → 1%, red sand → gold nuggets 12% → 2%, gravel → gold nuggets 5% → 1%, dirt → wheat seeds 10% → 3%. Sand no longer yields redstone. The soul sand table, gravel's coal/lapis/diamond, sand's kelp and quartz, and dirt's clay ball, bone meal and stick are unchanged; gravel can now yield redstone and red sand yields iron nuggets and raw iron. Size sieve farms for the new rates.

## New features

- **Gearbox** (all 12 woods) — an omnidirectional power hub: it passes power and spin on all six faces. Sources on different sides never jam, and an arm with no source of its own follows the nearest one. Split one input several ways, or turn a corner without a gear's counter-rotation; an omni machine (suction hopper, mechanical dispenser, piston) can draw from it on any face. **Craft:** a Mechanical Casing with gears on three sides and a shaft on top; the casing's wood picks the variant.
- **Chassis** (all 12 woods) — a casing with slime reach: it grabs every adjacent movable block except honey, so a contraption drags its payload with no glue brush, and still joins a same-wood frame. **Craft:** a Mechanical Casing ringed with iron nuggets.
- **Ratchet** — a one-way rotation valve: it passes rotation only while the line spins its allowed direction and freewheels (disconnects, no jam) the other way. Wrench to flip, sneak-wrench to inspect. Placed on a turning line it adopts that direction. While riding a contraption it passes power like a plain shaft. **Craft:** a bearing over a reverser over a gear, iron flanking the reverser, copper in the corners.
- **Propeller, Large propeller, Huge propeller** — ship thrust (5 / 10 / 20 power), crafted from the matching windmill, keeping its banner design. They do nothing in the world; a BlockShips vessel reads them. Mount direction picks the role: fore/aft adds speed, sideways adds turning, up or down adds lift. **Craft:** a windmill in the centre, iron corners, redstone edges.
- **Thruster** — burns fuel instead of rotation power, so it works on a ship with no network. It burns only while pushing the ship (throttle, steering, or lift holding a flier up); parked, docked or placed, it burns nothing. Its plume shows while it burns and follows the nozzle. A floor-mounted thruster still lifts, but its plume is hidden in the block below; hang lifting thrusters under the hull. **Craft:** a Fan, four iron ingots, two blaze rods and a blast furnace.
- **Reaction wheel** — a gyroscope that turns the ship, even at a standstill, for 1 power. Floor mount only, driven by a shaft underneath; it's the only turning a floor mount gives. **Craft:** two redstone either side of an iron block, over a bearing.
- **Wheel, Large wheel, Huge wheel** (all 12 woods) — land-vehicle wheels for BlockShips (2 / 5 / 10 power). A hull with wheels on its left and right drives on land, climbing 1 / 1.5 / 2-block steps by size; an unpowered wheel still turns with the vehicle's ground speed. Wall placement only; a wheel takes power through the block it hangs on, and only while its vehicle is assembled — a placed wheel draws no power and stays still. The catalog shows the oak wheels; every wood crafts the same way. **Craft:** four planks around a Rotator; a larger wheel is four planks around the size below, in the same wood.
- **Carried contraptions** — piston, rotator, drawbridge, minecart and chain hoist carry glued machines: a chain hoist glued to a rotator swings with its chain and platform, and a carried drill, placer or fan keeps aiming along its own facing.
- **Cushions, item frames and paintings ride along** — a 26.3 cushion keeps its rider seated through the move; frames keep their item and re-hang on the turned face; paintings re-hang too. With nowhere to land (a protected cell, a block in the way, a painting rolled onto its side) they drop as items.
- **Hoppers, furnaces, smokers, blast furnaces and brewing stands can be carried** (assembling one used to fail), keeping their own menu; shulker boxes open as shulker boxes and chiseled bookshelves keep their books.
- **Ceiling mounts** — a rotator under a block turns it about the vertical axis like a floor-mounted door; propellers and windmills of every tier hang under a block on a shaft running up into it, working exactly like a floor mount.
- **Frame blocks are wood-keyed** — casing, gearbox and chassis auto-glue only to same-wood frame blocks. The glue brush **pins** a frame to anything, any wood.
- **Glued selections up to 1024 blocks** (was 256).
- **Engines burn fuel for the power actually used** — a machine burns fuel only while it is working (a millstone, press or sieve processing, a drill boring, a pump pumping, a placer placing, a suction hopper catching items, a dispenser firing, a mechanical piston or chain hoist moving), so an idle line burns nothing, half load burns half as fast, and windmills and water wheels cover the load first. A fan counts whenever it is powered. Ship propellers, wheels and thrusters burn by how hard BlockShips uses them, and nothing on a docked ship. Steam pistons drink boiler water the same way.
- **Fuel settings are admin-editable** — copy the `fuel`, `engines`, `thruster`, `steam` or `load` sections of rotation-config.yml into `plugins/DefCoreLib/config/fuel.yml`. It's read at startup and isn't created for you; a `fuel:` section there replaces the whole fuel table.
- **Carried machines show their particles** — engine smoke and burner flame follow the moving block; an engine aboard that burns nothing shows no smoke (`engines: { idle-smoke: true }` in fuel.yml restores it).
- **Moving contraptions light up** with the optional DynLight plugin — torches, glowstone, lit candles and glowing heads keep lighting their surroundings in transit.
- **Mounted drill mines mid-swing** — a drill on a rotator bores through the whole arc, at 2× speed while swinging.
- **Wrench-inspect a moving network** — the wrench readout (supply, demand, direction, jam, highlighted members) works on a network riding a contraption.
- **Movers mow plants** — a mechanical piston or chain hoist breaks grass, flowers and similar blocks ahead of it instead of stalling.
- **The glue brush says how to start** — left-clicking with no session open tells you to right-click a hinge, piston, hoist or minecart first.
- **Smoother animations** — spinning parts tween between frames instead of snapping.
- **Catalog and help** — every mech block, with recipes, in `/defcorelib catalog`; `/mech` and `/mech help` show the commands and links (`/mech advance` still needs `mech.admin`).
- **Advancements** — "One-Way Street" (craft a Ratchet) and "Every Which Way" (craft a Gearbox).

## Fixes

- **Carried containers no longer duplicate their contents** — a moved chest's items were spilled into the world while a copy rode along, and a carried double chest landed with both halves' items in each half. Each half now carries and lands its own 27 slots, and a menu left open on a carried block closes when it moves or lands.
- **Pick-block no longer creates mech blocks** — middle-clicking a mech block or mechanism cart in survival or adventure selects one you carry instead of minting one (creative still gives it).
- **Mode menus no longer eat items** — dragging an item into a Redstone Dynamo or Rotator mode menu destroyed it on close.
- **Chain hoist reel-in dupe** — a rising hoist whose stroke was cut short could place a chain from nothing in a mined-out link; pulsing power repeated it.
- **Chain hoist broken while lowering** lost up to 47 chains; the unused chain now drops with the hoist.
- **Chain pulley glued into a mechanism duplicated chains**, and breaking one end while the other sat in an unloaded chunk could refund twice. Refunds no longer vanish when your inventory is full (the rest drops at your feet), and a pulley no longer grows another copy of its strand on every chunk load.
- **Placer emptied shulker boxes** — a filled shulker box was placed empty; banner patterns, head skins and other plugins' custom-block data were lost the same way, and doors, beds and tall plants were placed as one half. The placer now leaves those in its source (a plain custom name is still lost, as with vanilla placement).
- **Copper chest beside a Boiler or Water Wheel** joined it into a double chest; breaking the machine wiped the neighbour's contents. They no longer pair, and breaking one never touches the other's items.
- **Log out while seated on a contraption** and you're back in your seat when you rejoin, if it's still there; otherwise you rejoin where you left.
- **Mechanism minecarts can be broken while loaded** — an assembled cart ignored all damage; breaking it now lands its load and drops the cart. Right-click assembly asks your protection plugin.
- **Mechanism carts keep rolling** when they decline a load, and one bad cart can't strand the others' blocks at chunk unload or shutdown.
- **Contraptions no longer go through portals** — a mechanism minecart entering a portal left its hitboxes behind; it now stays put.
- **Machines in motion can't be grabbed** — a mover refuses to carry a rotator or door mid-swing, a piston mid-stroke or a moving hoist instead of tearing it apart. A carried hoist that would be tipped off its column is refused too, and its platform glue is cleared when its chain length changes out of band.
- **Blocks that can't land drop the right item** — a wall torch, sign, head or redstone wire dropped nothing, and a door or bed dropped two; they now drop one of themselves.
- **Carried blocks keep their contents and decoration** — a lectern's book, a decorated pot's item and sherds, sign text, a container's name, a jukebox's disc, a player head's skin; a mechanical dispenser's contents ride along instead of dropping.
- **Carried banners survive** — vanilla, large and huge banners keep their patterns, tier and orientation and re-land with the contraption, with no ghost copy.
- **Carried machines keep their settings** — a rotator keeps its angle, a redstone dynamo its mode, a throttle lever its level.
- **Drawbridges turn what they swing the right way** — a wall-mounted rotator landed its blocks as if it had turned flat, so stairs, logs, slabs, trapdoors, levers, shafts and gears came down facing the wrong way (a shaft could even join the wrong line, spinning backwards). Blocks now turn with the swing, and the glue brush accepts them on a drawbridge; a torch, sign, banner or head tipped onto a floor or wall takes its standing or wall form, and anything with no sideways form (a door, bed, plant, rail or carpet) drops as an item.
- **Moving contraptions leave no invisible walls** after part of their chunk unloads and reloads.
- **Carried frames, paintings and drops avoid fire, lava and portals** when they land.
- **Engines and burners keep their fuel** — a part-burned item survives being carried and restarts, and a landed engine no longer gets back the fuel it had at take-off.
- **A burned lava bucket or seed-oil bottle returns its container** — stacked onto one already in the machine, otherwise dropped beside it (an engine aboard keeps it in its storage instead of dropping it overboard).
- **Surplus power costs fuel** — a millstone or press running bigger batches with spare power pays for the extra cycles it actually ran, and a mechanical piston or chain hoist pays for the speed spare power gives it.
- **Redstone motors and clutches catch up on load** — redstone changed while their chunk was unloaded no longer leaves a motor running as a phantom source or a clutch stuck locked.
- **The suction hopper leaves protected items alone** (shop displays), and plugins can veto its pickups like a vanilla hopper's.
- **Steam piston parts go out when it's broken**, and its status readout no longer says "no fuel" while the burner holds fuel.
- **Machines at the edge of the loaded area** no longer load the chunk next door, and an idle, fully extended piston or resting hoist no longer re-checks its whole load every few ticks.
- **A parked contraption's drill stops cracking its block.**
- **A machine placed shortly before a crash** (water wheel, boiler, mechanical dispenser) is recognised again instead of coming back as a plain block.
- **Bare shafts stay bare** while carried and on landing.
- **Heads keep their facing on a moving mechanism** — wall heads rendered backwards, floor heads snapped to a default, and dynamos, boilers, burners, pumps, dispensers, piston heads and cart rails briefly showed the wrong direction in transit.
- **No blocks lost when stopping the server** with a contraption carrying a throttle lever assembled; a misleading shutdown warning is gone.
- **Server hang under pulse storms** — reactive and custom-rail updates coalesce to one recompute per tick, and a throwing handler is logged once instead of stalling the tick.
- **Light blocks no longer stop a piston or hoist.**
- **Chunk-boundary crash** when a mechanism minecart crossed a chunk boundary mid-tick.
- **Contraptions reliably survive a crash** — a parked mechanism (and a BlockShips ship) is rediscovered from its own entities when its chunk loads.
- **A rotator whose landing hit an error** no longer stays stuck "moving".
- **Glue can't be pointed at new blocks** — a chain hoist's glue on vanished blocks is dropped instead of grabbing whatever is at the new depth, and a glued cart moved off its cell loses its glue. Box-gluing only claim-checks the glued blocks, and a box may span at most 128 blocks horizontally, with every chunk loaded.
