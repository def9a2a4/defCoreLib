# railbound v0.5.0

The first release since 0.3.2 (0.4.0 was never published). Land-claim checks for carts, and fixes for trains, custom rails and cart contents. The carts and rails live in DefCoreLib, so these changes come from the core.

## Update notes

- **Needs DefCoreLib 0.5.0 or newer**, on Paper or Purpur 1.21.11 or newer.
- **Trains stop outside simulation distance**, as vanilla carts do. An unridden train used to keep driving and load chunks ahead of itself; it now pauses when its front cart leaves the active area and resumes where it stopped.

## New features

- **Land claims are respected** — placing a custom cart, assembling a mechanism cart, coupling and uncoupling, and opening a cart's inventory all ask your protection plugin.
- **Catalog** — the carts and rails show up, with recipes, in `/defcorelib catalog`.
- **Pick-block** selects a custom cart or rail you carry instead of minting one in survival or adventure (creative still gives it).

## Fixes

- **Breaking a cart inside a claim** where the break was refused still removed the cart and dropped its item and contents.
- **The dispenser cart can't drain sealed machines** — under a Redstone Dynamo it pulled out the dynamo's internal redstone, an endless supply. It now skips sealed containers and respects chest-lock plugins.
- **Cart contents are saved at every world save** — a crash could duplicate or lose items moved since the cart was last closed or unloaded.
- **Logging out on a cart** no longer duplicates its contents or brings the cart back broken.
- **Trains no longer stall the server** — once a coupled cart was gone (rider logged out, `/kill`, the void), every train restarted each tick and the broken one froze.
- **Custom rails drop as themselves when their support goes**, and placing a plain rail back no longer revives the custom one.
- **Hopper minecarts survive the destructor rail** instead of coming back as plain minecarts.
- **A cart parked on a powered activator rail** no longer fires again every time its chunk loads.
- **Coal tenders don't waste fuel** — an item is only taken when the engine has room.
- **Server hang under pulse storms** — a re-pulsed junction, controller or destructor rail is handled once per tick.
- A cart's light no longer lingers after it unloads.
