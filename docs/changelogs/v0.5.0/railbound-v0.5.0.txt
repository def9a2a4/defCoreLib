# railbound v0.5.0

The first release since 0.3.2 — 0.4.0 was prepared but never published. The carts and rails live in DefCoreLib, so these changes come from the core.

Needs DefCoreLib 0.5.0 or newer, on Paper 1.21.11 or newer.

- **Land claims are respected for carts** — placing a custom cart and right-click assembling a mechanism cart now ask your protection plugin first.
- **Land claims are respected when breaking carts.** Breaking a blast-furnace cart, coal tender or dispenser cart inside a claim where a protection plugin refused the break still removed the cart and dropped its item and contents. The refusal now wins.
- **Pick-block** — middle-clicking a custom cart or rail no longer mints a free copy in survival or adventure; it only selects one you already carry (creative still gives it).
- **Server hang under pulse storms** — a comparator or observer re-pulsing a junction, controller or destructor rail is now handled once per rail per tick instead of queuing a flood of redundant checks.
- **Catalog** — the carts and rails show up, with recipes, in the in-game `/defcorelib catalog`.
- **Custom rails drop as themselves when their support goes** — breaking the block under a junction, controller or destructor rail popped it off as a plain rail (losing the custom item), and placing a plain rail back there revived the custom one.
- **Hopper minecarts survive the destructor rail** — one sent through came back as a plain minecart.
- **Trains stop outside simulation distance**, as vanilla carts do. An unridden train kept driving and loaded chunks ahead of itself. A train pauses when its front cart leaves the active area and resumes where it stopped.
- **Cart contents are saved at every world save** — a crash could duplicate or lose items moved in or out since the cart was last closed or unloaded.
- **The dispenser cart can't drain sealed machines** — parked under a Redstone Dynamo it pulled out the dynamo's internal redstone, an endless supply. It now skips sealed containers and respects chest-lock plugins.
- **Logging out on a cart** no longer duplicates its contents or brings the cart back broken — the rider is stepped off as they leave.
- **Trains no longer stall the server or each other** — once a coupled cart was gone (a rider logged out, `/kill`, the void), every train on the server restarted each tick: none could start from a push, chains were re-spawned constantly, and the broken train froze.
- **A cart parked on a powered activator rail** no longer fires again every time its chunk loads (a dispenser cart shot one item; a mechanism cart re-assembled).
- **Coal tenders don't waste fuel** — an item is only taken when the engine has room for it.
- **Coupling and uncoupling respect land claims**, and so does opening a cart's inventory.
- A cart's light no longer lingers after it unloads.
