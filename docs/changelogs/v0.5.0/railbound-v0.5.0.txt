# Railbound v0.5.0

Land-claim checks for carts, and fixes for trains, custom rails and cart contents.

## Update notes

- **Needs DefCoreLib 0.5.0**, on Paper or Purpur 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 or 26.3.
- **Trains stop outside simulation distance**, as vanilla carts do. An unridden train used to keep driving and load chunks ahead of itself; it now pauses when its front cart leaves the active area and resumes when it comes back.

## New features

- **Land claims are respected** — placing a custom cart, coupling and uncoupling, and opening a cart's inventory all ask your protection plugin.
- **Catalog** — the carts and rails show up, with recipes, in `/defcorelib catalog`.

## Fixes

- **Pick-block no longer creates a custom cart or rail** in survival or adventure — it selects one you carry (creative still gives it).
- **Breaking a cart inside a claim** where the break was refused still removed the cart and dropped its item and contents. If the break is refused, its chains stay too.
- **The dispenser cart can't drain sealed machines** — under a Redstone Dynamo it pulled out the dynamo's internal redstone, an endless supply. It now skips sealed containers and respects chest-lock plugins.
- **Cart contents are saved at every world save** — a crash could duplicate or lose items moved since the cart was last closed or unloaded. This applies to every custom cart.
- **Logging out on a cart, or the server stopping while you ride one,** no longer duplicates its contents or brings the cart back broken — you're stepped off and rejoin standing beside it.
- **Trains no longer stall the server or each other** — once a coupled cart was gone (a rider logged out, `/kill`, the void), every train restarted each tick and the broken one froze. A train stopped at a controller rail now departs when it's powered again, even after its chunk or another train's has loaded or unloaded.
- **Custom rails drop as themselves when their support goes**, and placing a plain rail back no longer revives the custom one.
- **Hopper, chest and other special minecarts** sent over a destructor rail drop as their own item instead of a plain minecart.
- **A cart parked on a powered activator rail** no longer fires again every time its chunk loads.
- **Coal tenders don't waste fuel** — an engine fed a coal block threw away the burn above its cap; it now gets the whole burn, and smaller fuel is used first. A parked train no longer feeds its furnace minecart, which burned the coal standing still.
- **Server hang under pulse storms** — a re-pulsed junction, controller or destructor rail is handled once per tick.
- A lit blast cart's dynamic light no longer lingers after it unloads.
