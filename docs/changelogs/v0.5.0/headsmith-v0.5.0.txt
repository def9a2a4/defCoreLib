# headsmith v0.5.0

**HeadSmith is now a DefCoreLib companion plugin.** This is a rebuild, not an update to the standalone 0.1.4 — everything lives as DefCoreLib data under the `headsmith` namespace, so the heads register, craft, place, and break like any other def block and show up in the core item catalog. The version jumps to 0.5.0 to line up with the rest of the suite.

**It now needs DefCoreLib installed** (0.5.0 or newer, on Paper 1.21.11 or newer). Without the core, the heads simply don't load.

**Upgrading from the standalone?**

- Remove the old standalone HeadSmith jar first — both are named HeadSmith and can't be installed together.
- Its `config.yml` is no longer read: `excluded-tags`, `excluded-heads`, `custom-head-files` and `tag-order` have no effect.
- Heads you already placed are adopted automatically as their chunks load, and old head items convert when they land in an inventory. `/headsmith migrate` only re-runs the sweep over loaded chunks by hand.
- Placed heads are recognized by their skin alone (the old plugin marked nothing else on them), so a head from another plugin that uses the exact same skin, already placed in the world, is taken over as a HeadSmith head too and drops as one. This keeps happening as chunks load, not only once at upgrade: a same-skin head from another plugin (HeadDatabase, a head shop) placed at any time becomes a HeadSmith head the next time its chunk loads, and then drops as a HeadSmith item without its original name or lore. Head *items* are matched by the old plugin's own marker, so an item in an inventory is never converted.

## Heads

- A big catalog of decorative heads organized into families: the alphabet (letters, numbers, greek, cyrillic, runes, galactic, zodiac, arrows, and symbols across many materials), mini-blocks, candles, books, barrels, bundles, buckets, pumpkins, chalices, bottles, and more.
- A cross-cutting color axis groups every black / white / red / … head under its color, so you can browse by shape or by color.
- Browse them all in-game with the core catalog: `/defcorelib catalog` groups every HeadSmith head by category, and each dye-color node shows that color's mini-wool as its icon.
- Get them in survival by crafting: most heads are carved in the stonecutter from mini-blocks (for example, a mini birch-planks block cuts into birch letters), with some shaped and shapeless recipes too.
- HeadSmith recipes stay out of the vanilla recipe book by default. Run `/headsmith recipes give` to reveal them in your book, or `take` to hide them again.

## Chimney heads (new)

- Chimney heads in a range of materials (stone, brick, cobblestone, sandstone, nether brick, metal, and more). Light one with flint & steel and it puffs a steady rising campfire-smoke plume; right-click again to snuff it out with an extinguish sound. Chimneys give off smoke only — they emit no light.
- Chimneys have a stonecutter recipe like the rest.

## Commands

- `/headsmith` or `/headsmith help` — what HeadSmith does, the commands you may use, and the GitHub and Discord links.
- `/headsmith give <id> [amount] [player]` — give a head by id, to yourself or another player (op).
- `/headsmith recipes <give|take> [player]` — reveal or hide HeadSmith recipes in a recipe book (anyone may run it for themselves; running it for another player needs `headsmith.recipes.others`, operators by default).
- `/headsmith migrate` — re-run the adoption sweep for already-placed heads from the old standalone HeadSmith plugin over loaded chunks (op; adoption otherwise happens automatically).
