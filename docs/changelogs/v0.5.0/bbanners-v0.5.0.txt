# bbanners v0.5.0

The first release since 0.3.2 — 0.4.0 was prepared but never published. The banner logic lives in DefCoreLib, so these changes come from the core.

Needs DefCoreLib 0.5.0 or newer, on Paper 1.21.11 or newer.

- **Land claims are respected for banners.** Fence flags, bed banners and large/huge banners are display entities rather than blocks, so placing or shearing them skipped protection plugins entirely. Both now ask first, and a refused player can't place or shear inside someone else's claim.
- **Banners ride mechanisms** — large and huge banners (and vanilla ones) on a mechanism now keep their patterns, tier and orientation as it moves and re-land with the contraption, instead of vanishing when it's taken apart. See the mech notes for details.
