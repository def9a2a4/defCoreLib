# BetterBanners v0.5.0

Land-claim checks for banners, and fixes for carried and protected banners.

## Update notes

- **Needs DefCoreLib 0.5.0**, on Paper or Purpur 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 or 26.3.

## New features

- **Land claims are respected** — fence flags, bed banners and large/huge banners are display entities, so placing or shearing them skipped protection plugins. Both now ask first.

## Fixes

- **Carried banners keep their patterns, tier and orientation** and re-land with the contraption, with no ghost copy; after a crash, a re-landed contraption puts back banners that hadn't reached disk. See the mech notes.
- **Banners stay put when a protection plugin refuses a break** — they used to drop even when the block they hung on was kept.
- **Wind charges and breezes** no longer knock banners down.
