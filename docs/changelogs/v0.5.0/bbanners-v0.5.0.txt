# bbanners v0.5.0

The first release since 0.3.2 (0.4.0 was never published). Banners ride mechanisms and respect land claims. The banner logic lives in DefCoreLib, so these changes come from the core.

## Update notes

- **Needs DefCoreLib 0.5.0 or newer**, on Paper or Purpur 1.21.11 or newer.

## New features

- **Banners ride mechanisms** — vanilla, large and huge banners on a contraption keep their patterns, tier and orientation as it moves and re-land with it. See the mech notes.
- **Land claims are respected** — fence flags, bed banners and large/huge banners are display entities, so placing or shearing them skipped protection plugins. Both now ask first.

## Fixes

- **Banners stay put when a protection plugin refuses a break** — they used to drop even when the block they hung on was kept.
- **Wind charges and breezes** no longer knock banners down.
