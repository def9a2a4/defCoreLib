# DefCoreLib v0.5.0

The first release since 0.3.2 (0.4.0 was never published, so everything here is new to you). Contraptions now carry glued machines, cushions, item frames and paintings; new frame blocks, a ratchet, and ship propulsion and wheels for BlockShips; an in-game item catalog; land-claim checks across the suite; and a long list of item-loss, dupe and crash fixes.

## Update notes

- **Runs on Paper or Purpur 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3** (was 1.21.8). `api-version` is still `1.21`, so an older server loads the plugins and fails at runtime instead of refusing them at startup.
- **Update every def plugin together.** The companions need DefCoreLib 0.5.0, and BlockShips needs 0.0.18 or newer alongside this release.
- **Contraptions move slower** — speed now follows the load's mass, and the rotator, hoist and piston speed limits came down. Details in the mech notes.
- **Bare shafts become waxed copper chains** (were iron chains). Existing worlds convert as chunks load, keeping each shaft's axis.
- **Carried windmills supply half power** (0 / 2 / 7 instead of 1 / 5 / 15), and **power blocks weigh something on a ship**. A contraption or ship near its power, float or lift limit may need rebalancing. Details in the mech notes.
- **Frames only drag frames, and mixed-wood frames no longer auto-glue.** Glue-brush the joints of an existing contraption that relied on either.
- **Mechanism recipes and sieve drop rates changed** — see the mech notes.
- **Pipes, HeadSmith and Railbound have their own upgrade notes** — Pipes migrates its config.yml, splits its permissions and drops `/pipes recipes`; HeadSmith replaces the standalone jar and its commands; trains now stop outside simulation distance.

## New features

- **Carried contraptions, new blocks and ceiling mounts** — movers carry nested glued machines as one rigid body; cushions, item frames and paintings ride along; Gearbox, Chassis, Ratchet, three Propellers, Thruster, Reaction wheel and three sizes of Wheel; rotators, propellers and windmills can hang under a block; engines burn fuel only for the power used, with admin overrides in `config/fuel.yml`; glued selections up to 1024 blocks. (mech plugin)
- **Ships are saved with the world** — a BlockShips ship (any persisted contraption) is rediscovered from its own entities when its chunk loads; a damaged save is never deleted, recovery pauses with one warning until the file is repaired or moved aside. Other contraptions aren't saved and land when their chunk unloads or the server stops.
- **Filter pipes** — three tiers of item filter. (pipes plugin)
- **HeadSmith joins the suite** as a DefCoreLib companion. (headsmith plugin)
- **In-game item catalog** — `/defcorelib catalog` browses every registered block across the def plugins, grouped by category, with search and recipe drill-down. Open to everyone (`corelib.catalog`, default on). Admins (`corelib.admin`) can right-click an entry, or the item in its detail view, to give themselves one.
- **Help for every command** — `/defcorelib`, `/mech`, `/pipes` and `/headsmith` show what the plugin does, the subcommands you may use, and the GitHub and Discord links. An unknown subcommand shows the help. Each `/defcorelib` subcommand checks its own permission (`catalog` is open; the rest need `corelib.admin`).
- **`give` can target a player** — `/defcorelib give`, `/pipes give` and `/headsmith give` take `<id> [amount] [player]`, work from the console, and drop what doesn't fit at the player's feet. The amount is capped at one stack (an unstackable item comes as 1), and an id several plugins share asks for the namespace.
- **Land-claim checks** — gluing, placing carts and banners, coupling carts, assembling cart mechanisms by hand, opening custom containers (GriefPrevention container trust, WorldGuard chest-access, Towny switch rights), lighting heads and placing pipes against claimed containers all ask your protection plugin. Engines, burners and suction hoppers open their storage without the container check. DefCoreLib soft-depends on GriefPrevention, WorldGuard, Towny, Lands, PlotSquared and Residence so it sees their verdict whatever the load order, and its test placements are invisible to block loggers (CoreProtect, Prism).
- **`/defcorelib debug [on|off]`** — verbose logging for DefCoreLib, Pipes and HeadSmith, saved in the new `plugins/DefCoreLib/config.yml`. `/defcorelib refreshdisplays` reports stale block displays, and with `confirm` respawns them.
- **The BetterBanners tier gate only gates recipes** — without BetterBanners the large and huge windmills are uncraftable, but existing ones keep working and still craft into the matching tier propeller.

### For plugin developers

- **Behaviour changes that can affect you:**
  - `MechanismAssembleEvent` also fires for crash-recovered mechanisms; check `isRecovered()`.
  - `MechanismRegistry.persist()` throws `UncheckedIOException` when the save can't be written.
  - A `DropItemHook` now receives the item form of wall variants instead of `null`.
  - Block YAML `sound` values are namespaced keys (`block.wood.break`); enum names like `BLOCK_WOOD_BREAK` no longer load.
  - A placeable block needs a `texture` unless it sets `placeable: false`; it now fails at load.
  - `MechanismBlockData.blockData` can be `null` (block-free parts from `assembleFromParts`).
  - `Mechanism` gained abstract methods: implement it only through DefCoreLib.
- **Rotation state (experimental):** `Mechanism.isRotationPowered(int)`, `rotationSurplus(int)` (-1 for a non-node; one value for the whole network), `rotationFacing(int)` (mechanism-local aim), and `CoreLibPlugin.rotationFacingAt(Block)` for a placed block. `MechanismRotationSolvedEvent` fires when a mechanism's network re-solves (at most every 20 ticks) and once at assembly.
- **Driving and persistence:** driven `assembleMechanism(…, existingVehicle, …)` with `repositionDriven`; `persist(Mechanism)`, `hasPersistedState`, `discardPersistedState(World, UUID)`, `isRecoveryPending(UUID)`, `byId`, `activeMechanisms()`; `MechanismTeardownRedoEvent` fires when a crash-undone landing is redone.
- **Ship-building:** seats (`designateSeat`, `isSeat`, `seatEntity`, `setSeatOccupant`, `SeatListener`); colliders (`colliderCount`, `getColliderBoxByBlock`, `colliderEntity`, `colliderBoxes()`); `vehicle()`, `totalMass()`, `blockMass(int)`; `assembleFromParts(PartSpec…)`; `setBlockDemand` (0 when unset), `isEngineFuelled`, `setBlockSpinScale`, `setBlockSpinSpeed`, `clearBlockSpin`; `MechanismRegistry.fuelSecondsLeft(Mechanism)` (-1 with no engines, +∞ when none is burning).
- **Landing seams:** `addPreAirOutListener`, `CellPlacePolicy` / `PlaceDecision`, `DropItemHook`, `setBeforeEntityRemoval`, `landingSolidWouldWin(Material)`, and the `BlockSnapshotProvider` SPI (`registerBlockSnapshotProvider`). A landed block whose type isn't registered keeps foreign PDC keys.
- **Glue and protection:** `registerAnchorProvider` / `ExternalAnchor`, `openGlueSession`, `readGlueOffsets`, `writeGlueOffsets`, `resolveGlue`, `clearGlue`, `glueMaxSize`; `BuildProbe.mayBuild` / `isProbing`; `ContainerAccess.mayOpen(Player, Location)`.
- **Blocks and catalog:** `requires_plugin` recipe gate; recipe `output:` override; `furnace:` and `smoking:` recipes; a version-gated ingredient skips just that recipe; particle `velocity`; Builder hooks `onEntitiesLoad`, `onCapture`, `onRestore`; `registerCatalogContribution(CatalogContribution)` and the YAML `categories:` field.
- **Other:** `bannerTiersIn(World, BoundingBox)` — large/huge banner displays by host block (expand the region by at least 3 blocks, only chunks with loaded entities are searched, and `BannerTier.NORMAL` means fence flags and bed banners); `massRegistry()`; `CushionSeats` (experimental, empty answers before 26.3); `CommandHelp` for companion help, usage, tab completion and `give`; `ContainerAdapter.peekExtract`; `withPhysicsSuppressed`; `isCapturingForMechanism`; `BlockRotation.Turn` with `rotateBlockData(BlockData, Turn)`; `DebugLog`.
- `CoreLibPlugin.API_LEVEL` — 1 in 0.5.0 (everything since 0.3.2); a missing field means 0. It rises whenever consumers can rely on something new.
- `MachineProducedEvent` now reports the full amount produced when the output merges into a partly full stack.

## Fixes

- **Ships survive a crash right after docking.** A persisted contraption (a BlockShips ship) deleted its save the moment it was taken apart, before the world had saved the placed blocks. Its save is now replaced by a record of the landing; after a crash the next boot finishes the landing from that record, leaving blocks that did reach disk alone.
- **Ships come back where they are after a crash.** Recovery now centres on the vehicle it finds instead of the last saved position. A second copy of the frame or colliders left by a crash is removed, and missing colliders are spawned again.
- **Cargo is saved with the world** and when a player logs out with it open, so after a crash cargo matches players' inventories.
- **Pick-block no longer creates custom blocks** — middle-clicking a custom block or cart in survival or adventure selects one you carry instead of minting one (creative still gives it).
- **Older custom items work in recipes again** — items made before their description changed (0.3.2 casings, rotators, fans, chain pulleys, corner pipes…) are refreshed when you open an inventory, join or pick them up.
- **Custom blocks stay intact when a protection plugin refuses a break or explosion** — they used to drop their item and contents anyway, duplicating them.
- **Machine contents are saved within a second** of changing, instead of only at chunk unload.
- **Ship cargo dupe** — a cargo menu left open while the ship's chunk unloaded let a player take items that came back on reload. The menu now closes.
- **Unreadable containers are sealed** — a custom container or ship cargo whose saved contents can't be read (say, after removing a datapack) won't open and is never saved over; the data is kept as it was and a warning is logged.
- **One unreadable block no longer stops a saved ship from coming back** — a carried block whose saved form no longer loads (say, after a datapack or version change) comes back as that block's plain default, or air if the block is gone, with a warning; the ship used to stay stuck unrecovered.
- **Unloading a world** (Multiverse and the like) saves and releases its custom blocks and containers, and lands any moving contraption or loaded mechanism cart; reloading the world could duplicate or lose container items.
- **Custom blocks placed shortly before a crash are recognised again** — a water wheel, boiler or mechanical dispenser could come back as a plain vanilla block.
- **Refused placements leave nothing behind** — a refused custom placement left an invisible marker that turned the next matching vanilla block into the custom one.
- **Pistons pop custom heads instead of stripping them** — a pushed decorative head, vertical slab or indicator now drops as its own item (or its usual block drop) with its storage, instead of moving as a plain player head.
- **Wind charges and breezes leave custom blocks alone** — their gusts broke custom heads and spilled their storage, even inside claims.
- **The stonecutter menu pages** — an input with more than 45 custom outputs showed only the first 45.
- **Server hang under pulse storms** — the rotation network and custom rails coalesce recomputes to once per tick, and the plugin's own block writes no longer feed their own physics loop.
- **The catalog is cheap to browse** — it sorts once and builds only the page you're looking at.
- Reloading a companion plugin (PlugMan) scans loaded chunks once, not once per block type.
- Stopping or reloading the server with a storage menu open no longer logs an error for each one.
- Chunk unloads, hopper transfers beside custom blocks and the persisted-contraption world-save snapshot cost less.

See the per-plugin notes (mech, pipes, railbound, headsmith, bbanners, rsd, vslab) for their details; contraption and machine fixes are in the mech notes.
