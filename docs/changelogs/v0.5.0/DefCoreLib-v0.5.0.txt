# DefCoreLib v0.5.0

The first release since 0.3.2 (0.4.0 was never published, so everything here is new to you). Contraptions now carry glued machines, cushions, item frames and paintings; new frame blocks, a ratchet, and ship propulsion and wheels for BlockShips; an in-game item catalog; land-claim checks across the suite; and a long list of item-loss, dupe and crash fixes.

## Update notes

- **Runs on Paper or Purpur 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3** (was 1.21.8). Mechanism needs 1.21.11 or newer. `api-version` is still `1.21`, so an older server loads the plugin and fails at runtime instead of refusing it at startup.
- **Update BlockShips to 0.0.18 or newer** alongside this release; DefCoreLib warns at startup about an older one.
- **Bare shafts become waxed copper chains** (were iron chains). Existing worlds convert as chunks load, keeping each shaft's axis.
- **Carried windmills supply half power** (0 / 2 / 7 instead of 1 / 5 / 15), and **power blocks weigh something on a ship**. A contraption or ship near its power, float or lift limit may need rebalancing. Details in the mech notes.
- **Mixed-wood casing frames no longer auto-glue.** Rebuild a frame in one wood, or brush-pin the mixed joints.
- **Mechanism recipes and sieve drop rates changed** — see the mech notes.

## New features

- **Carried contraptions** — movers (piston, rotator, drawbridge, minecart, chain hoist) carry nested glued machines as one rigid body: a chain hoist glued to a rotator swings with its chain and platform. (mech plugin)
- **Cushions, item frames and paintings ride along** — a 26.3 cushion keeps its rider seated through the move; frames keep their item and re-hang turned with the block; paintings re-hang too. With nowhere to land they drop as items. Cushion support switches itself off before 26.3. (mech plugin)
- **New blocks** — Gearbox, Chassis, Ratchet, three Propellers, Thruster, Reaction wheel, and three sizes of Wheel. (mech plugin)
- **Ceiling mounts** — rotators, propellers and windmills can hang under a block. (mech plugin)
- **Engines burn fuel for the power actually used**, and fuel settings are admin-editable in `config/fuel.yml`. (mech plugin)
- **Glued selections up to 1024 blocks** (was 256).
- **Filter pipes** — three tiers of item filter. (pipes plugin)
- **HeadSmith joins the suite** as a DefCoreLib companion. (headsmith plugin)
- **In-game item catalog** — `/defcorelib catalog` browses every registered block across the def plugins, grouped by category, with search and recipe drill-down. Open to everyone (`corelib.catalog`, default on); admins can right-click to give.
- **Help for every command** — `/defcorelib`, `/mech`, `/pipes` and `/headsmith` show what the plugin does, the subcommands you may use, and the GitHub and Discord links. An unknown subcommand shows the help.
- **`give` can target a player** — `/defcorelib give`, `/pipes give` and `/headsmith give` take `<id> [amount] [player]`, work from the console, and drop what doesn't fit at the player's feet.
- **Land-claim checks** — gluing, placing carts and banners, assembling cart mechanisms, opening custom containers (GriefPrevention container trust, WorldGuard chest-access, Towny switch rights), lighting heads and pipes against claimed containers all ask your protection plugin. DefCoreLib soft-depends on GriefPrevention, WorldGuard, Towny, Lands, PlotSquared and Residence so it sees their verdict whatever the load order, and its test placements are invisible to block loggers (CoreProtect, Prism).
- **Pick-block** no longer mints a free copy of a custom block or cart in survival or adventure; it only selects one you carry. Creative is unchanged.
- **The BetterBanners tier gate only gates recipes** — without BetterBanners the large and huge windmills are uncraftable, but existing ones keep working and convert to tier propellers tier-for-tier.

### For plugin developers

- Live rotation state: `isRotationPowered(int)`, `rotationSurplus(int)`, `rotationFacing(int)`, `rotationFacingAt(Block)`. `powered`, `surplus` and `jammed` are per network, identical for every member.
- `MechanismRotationSolvedEvent` — fires when a mechanism's network re-solves (at most every 20 ticks).
- `bannerTiersIn(World, BoundingBox)` — large/huge banner displays by host block. Expand the region by at least 3 blocks (a banner's display sits in the neighbour cell), only chunks with loaded entities are searched (gate on `Chunk.isEntitiesLoaded()`), and `BannerTier.NORMAL` means fence flags and bed banners, never banner blocks.
- `massRegistry()` — the mass table, to price blocks your own config doesn't list.
- `requires_plugin` recipe gate — withhold a block's recipes until a named plugin enables.
- `CushionSeats` (experimental) — 26.3 cushions for plugins that seat players themselves; empty answers before 26.3.
- `fuelSecondsLeft(Mechanism)` — seconds until the first burning engine aboard runs dry.
- `MechanismTeardownRedoEvent` — fires when a crash-undone landing is redone.
- `CommandHelp` — shared help, usage, tab completion and `give` for companion plugins.
- `CoreLibPlugin.API_LEVEL` — a feature level, raised whenever consumers can rely on something new (every 0.5.0 build reports the same version).
- `MachineProducedEvent` now reports the full amount produced when the output merges into a partly full stack.

## Fixes

- **Ships survive a crash right after docking.** A persisted contraption (a BlockShips ship) deleted its save the moment it was taken apart, before the world had saved the placed blocks. Its save is now replaced by a record of the landing; after a crash the next boot finishes the landing from that record, leaving blocks that did reach disk alone.
- **Ships come back where they are after a crash.** Recovery now centres on the vehicle it finds instead of the last saved position. A second copy of the frame or colliders left by a crash is removed, and missing colliders are spawned again.
- **Contraptions reliably survive a crash** — a parked mechanism is rediscovered from its own entities when its chunk loads.
- **Cargo is saved with the world** and when a player logs out with it open, so after a crash cargo matches players' inventories.
- **Machine contents are saved within a second** of changing, instead of only at chunk unload.
- **Ship cargo dupe** — a cargo menu left open while the ship's chunk unloaded let a player take items that came back on reload. The menu now closes.
- **Unreadable containers are never overwritten** — a custom container or ship cargo whose saved contents can't be read (say, after removing a datapack) is left untouched and logged, instead of saved back empty.
- **Unloading a world** (Multiverse and the like) saves and releases its custom blocks and containers; reloading it could duplicate or lose container items.
- **Custom blocks placed shortly before a crash are recognised again** — a water wheel, boiler or mechanical dispenser could come back as a plain vanilla block.
- **Refused placements leave nothing behind** — a refused custom placement left an invisible marker that turned the next matching vanilla block into the custom one.
- **Pistons no longer strip custom heads** — a pushed decorative head, vertical slab or indicator turned into a plain player head, losing its item, light and storage.
- **Wind charges and breezes leave custom blocks alone** — their gusts broke custom heads and spilled their storage, even inside claims.
- **Glue can't be pointed at new blocks** — a chain hoist's glue on vanished blocks is dropped instead of grabbing whatever is at the new depth, and a glued cart pushed off its cell loses its glue.
- **Box-gluing is bounded** — only the glued blocks are claim-checked (a big box fired tens of thousands of checks), and a box must be under 128 blocks across with every chunk loaded.
- **Moving contraptions leave no invisible walls** after part of their chunk unloads and reloads.
- **Carried frames, paintings and drops avoid fire, lava and portals** when they land.
- **Server hang under pulse storms** — the rotation network and custom rails coalesce recomputes to once per tick, and the plugin's own block writes no longer feed their own physics loop.
- **Wall heads on mechanisms no longer render backwards.**
- A rotator whose landing hit an error no longer stays stuck "moving".
- Reloading a companion plugin (PlugMan) scans loaded chunks once, not once per block type.
- `give` never hands out more than a stack's size in one stack.
- Mover speeds are steadier; chunk unloads, hopper transfers beside custom blocks and the persisted-contraption world-save snapshot cost less.

See the per-plugin notes (mech, pipes, railbound, headsmith, bbanners, rsd, vslab) for their details.
