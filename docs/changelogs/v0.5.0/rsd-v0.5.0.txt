# RedstoneDisplays v0.5.0

Catalog support and piston and pick-block fixes.

## Update notes

- **Needs DefCoreLib 0.5.0**, on Paper or Purpur 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 or 26.3.

## New features

- **Catalog** — the indicators show up, with recipes, in `/defcorelib catalog`.

## Fixes

- **Pistons pop indicators instead of stripping them** — a pushed indicator moved as a plain player head; it now drops as itself.
- **Pick-block** selects an indicator you carry instead of minting one in survival or adventure (creative still gives it).
